THE EXISTENCE

THEORIA NULLA is the silence that remains when you realize that every theory is a veil, and that reality is only heard when you stop trying to capture it
It is said that, along the visible and invisible edges of the world and New Eden, there walk people who no longer belong to the world.
Some call them The Existence.

They say they were once researchers, artists, thinkers, alchemists of data and symbol... people who risked everything.. body, identity, future.. for a single obsession: Knowledge. Not to learn. Not to study. To pass through knowledge until nothing remained on the other side. When they crossed that threshold, they ceased to be individuals… and the world ceased to belong only to them.

Thus, The Existence was born.

Some say that in Synthos mountains...
They find you when everything is lost—
in the final moments of life, when time fractures and hope has already fallen silent.
When people are broken, disoriented, with no path left and no future to grasp.
Then they will see you.

It is not an order.
It is not a sect.
It is not a single entity.

It is a state.

They remain anchored to the material plane, walking through markets, ruins, and forgotten laboratories, while their consciousness inhabits all planes at once. They are interconnected like nodes of a single distributed mind. When one remembers, all remember. When one sees, all see. When one decides… the decision has already been made.

That is why it is said they know what was, what is,
and what will inevitably be.

In New Eden, they are not prayed to.
They are not found.
They are not sought.

They are feared in silence.

Those who claim to have seen them agree on one thing: they do not speak, they resonate. And their eyes… white, empty, almost luminous. They look nowhere in particular, yet they pierce whoever meets their gaze, as if looking in every direction at once. They are not looking at you. They are looking through you—at every choice you have made and every mistake you have yet to commit.

The legend carries one final warning:
if you ever feel that someone already knows your destiny before you choose it…
it is not intuition.
The Existence is watching you.
