# Mega Ultra Master Plan v250

## Objective

Bring the complete access-to-portal sequence to one cinematic quality bar while
preserving the approved original portal vortex and all existing interaction
contracts.

## Art-direction decisions

1. The preportal fluid remains an independent anticipation layer.
2. The flat cyan bridge ring is replaced by a broken thin-film aperture with
   spectral refraction and organic edge motion.
3. The original Eden portal canvases, vortex particle field and choreography
   remain structurally untouched.
4. Embedded VEIL restores the standalone reference's three-octave motion,
   continuous cloth physics and optical frame persistence, then retains the
   integrated electricity, sparks, pulses, cursor gravity and portal lensing.
5. Transition sound gains a pressure-wave layer and phase-synchronous cues.
6. Warp camera shake becomes temporally coherent and the speedline canvas is
   high-DPI without per-particle gradient allocation.
7. Flat dashed portal/warp rings are replaced by restrained spectral optics.
8. Reduced-motion flash limiting and WebGL context-loss fallback remain part of
   the production contract.

## Performance contract

- One existing VEIL animation loop; no duplicate background renderer.
- Adaptive secondary density remains controlled by measured refresh cadence.
- Preportal WebGL renders only inside its anticipation envelope.
- Warp speedlines allocate no gradients in the hot particle loop.
- Primary VEIL and portal geometry retain native DPR; only optically fungible
  secondary buffers use bounded resolution.

## Cross-platform archetypal audit

### The Renderer — DNA molecular demo

The profile demo proves the value of data-oriented rendering: typed arrays,
`BufferGeometry`, point clouds, cached materials and instanced draw calls. KPR
already uses the same genetics in portal GPU particles and VEIL buffers. We also
identified patterns not to inherit: `preserveDrawingBuffer`, forced-disabled
frustum culling, per-frame geometry recreation and random camera shake.

### The Systems Engineer — INVICTVS

INVICTVS establishes the correct rule for approved imagery: native DPR is
sacred; performance must come from lowering invisible or optically fungible
work. KPR therefore retains native VEIL resolution while its bloom buffer stays
half-resolution, its hidden phases sleep and its portrait-blocked portal loop
does no simulation work.

### The Symbolic Architect — FRUGAL / Synkronia

The symbolic lesson is phase authority: every expensive effect belongs to one
explicit state and receives no compute outside it. The portal sequence keeps
separate access, fold, fluid, VEIL, portal and warp domains rather than letting
all loops coexist. Visual meaning and compute ownership share the same symbol.

### The Runtime Guardian — THESTRAL

The interface runtime favors event-driven updates and low-frequency telemetry.
KPR applies that principle by caching the settled VEIL activation field,
coalescing pointer work, moving sound envelopes to the audio thread and keeping
quality telemetry outside hot loops.

## Applied FPS findings without visual downgrade

- Fixed the portal stage to the actual viewport. The previous 1634 px inherited
  archive height was simulating and painting far outside a 900 px screen.
- Restored native DPR and reference VEIL motion simultaneously.
- Added a desynchronized 2D context hint for lower presentation latency.
- Cached settled node activation instead of recomputing it every frame.
- Batched the VEIL lattice into four opacity buckets, preserving every node and
  its glow while replacing thousands of individual sprite submissions with a
  handful of Canvas2D path fills (the DNA demo's instancing lesson).
- Put stale cursor-trail forces to sleep after their visible reaction window,
  eliminating dormant distance calculations while keeping live interaction exact.
- Stopped portal simulation while the mandatory portrait guard is active.
- Removed 10,000+ transient gradient allocations per second from warp streaks.
- Replaced frame-random camera shake with continuous harmonic motion.
- Retained half-resolution bloom because blur is optically invariant at that
  scale; primary geometry, portal silhouettes and particle density are intact.

## Validation gates

- Original portal canvas and GPU layer remain present.
- No legacy quantum iris is reintroduced.
- Reference VEIL motion signatures are present.
- Spectral aperture stylesheet is loaded last.
- Audio, reduced-motion and context-loss safeguards are connected.
- Production path contains no machine-specific absolute paths.
